## Spectral, residual and spatial definitions

For molecular geometry, normalize each candidate column, take the equal-weight mean over its aliases, then normalize this direction. Full cosine is the pairwise dot product. Reduced cosine divides every channel by the square root of its molecular occupancy before renormalizing. Occupancy counts distinct identities with positive support, not aliases. This shared-channel downweighting does not assert biochemical diagnostic fragments. Save nearest/top5 under both metrics, same/cross-class nearest and shared-fragment intersection/union statistics, with deterministic tie flags.

Use existing physical component and channel information for fragment support. Exclusive fragment-support channels have full-A molecular occupancy one; common support has occupancy greater than one. Count other identities' precursor support as well as fragment support in occupancy, preventing a precursor overlap from being called exclusive. These terms are statistical, not verified structural specificity. For each support set, common/specific support is the sum of observed b on its channels, in unchanged units without normalization. Preserve original rule_sheet/formula/component labels, and use `DIAGNOSTIC_RULE_NOT_VERIFIED` for missing biochemical rules. Do not invent a class headgroup or acyl-fragment rule. Observation claims require the unchanged verified observation rule; otherwise mark formal observed fractions unavailable. A separately named secondary `positive_B_fraction` may report the fraction of support channels with `b>0`; this records positive observed values and is not verified detection evidence or a replacement observation threshold.

Attribution uses actual candidate contributions: `attr_jf=sum(c in aliases(j)) x_c*A_fc / (sum(c) x_c*A_fc +1e-12)`. It does not use the equal-average geometry direction. Save mean/max and specific-support mean with explicit support-set counts; empty sets are unavailable.

Use signed residual `r=b-Ax` and `delta_r=r_deleted-r_full`. Save both `cos(r_deleted,a_k)` and the main residual-direction quantity `cos(delta_r,a_k)` with epsilon1e-12, plus near-zero-direction flags. **NNLS KKT makes retained active-column residual projections approximately zero; delta-residual projections are also constrained. A near-zero value does not prove absent competition.** Report these limitations. Separately compute removed source signal `s_j=sum(source aliases) A_c*x_full_c` and positive replacement signal `h_j=sum(other candidates) A_c*max(0,x_deleted_c-x_full_c)`, their cosine and projection coefficient. The old numerical diagnostic used the opposite residual sign and lacks full redistribution coefficients, so it cannot serve as a complete replacement cache.
