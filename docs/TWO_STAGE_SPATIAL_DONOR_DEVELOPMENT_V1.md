## One new feature family

For each molecular name g, sum **all** same-name candidate X_hat maps into s_g over the original foreground. This feature aggregation does not change the original reporting gate or molecular result aggregation. All 391 candidates contribute to the donor universe, grouped into the original 377 names. Only other-name donors h with mean(s_h)>mean(s_g) qualify. The larger-mean condition is a frozen heuristic motivated by weak false allocations copying stronger sources, not an abundance prior or truth test.

U1(g) is the minimum normalized squared residual when approximating s_g by one qualifying donor with a nonnegative coefficient. U2(g) is the corresponding minimum using at most two qualifying donors. Include single-donor boundaries, so 0<=U2<=U1<=1. No qualifying donor gives U1=U2=1; zero target maps are recorded explicitly and do not enter the classifier unless originally reported. Use float64 Gram calculations and a stable original-vector fallback for ill-conditioned pairs; no regularization or outcome-dependent donor limit. The two continuous classifier features are log10(max(U1,1e-12)) and log10(max(U2,1e-12)). Floors are numerical guards. Best donor names are audit metadata, never model inputs.

Rationale: experiment-wide systematic spectral mismatch may produce false coefficient maps proportional to one or more true source maps. The old auxiliary screen examined only spatial concentration, not relationships between full candidate maps. NNLS spectral KKT constraints do not force these row relationships. However, truly co-localized or shared-template identities can also have small U1/U2, and false allocations can involve more than two sources. No low-U hard veto, identity blacklist, calibrated-probability interpretation or claim of new independent observations.
